# EMCOR Group (emcor)

EMCOR Group is a Fortune 500 mechanical and electrical construction, energy, and facilities services company headquartered in Norwalk, Connecticut. It operates through three segments, EMCOR Construction Services, EMCOR Building Services, and EMCOR Industrial Services, delivering hyperscale data center construction, hospital support services, energy efficiency programs, and green building solutions.
